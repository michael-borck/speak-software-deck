01The Department

The intern — Confidence and correctness are different qualities. The intern has only the first.

The repository rule — If it is not in the repository, it does not exist.

The specialist — Re-explaining is the tax. The context folder abolishes it.

The filing cabinet — The most sophisticated AI orchestration is a well-organised folder.

The mini CEO's four duties — Delegation is not absolution.

02Speak

The spec is the program — Prompting is improvisation. Specification is authorship.

Done means — Done is undefined until it is runnable.

The hostile read — Every gesture-word is a gap.

Wishes into checks — The whole discipline is turning wishes into checks.

What you said, what you meant — The AI knows what you said. It does not know what you meant.

03Verify

The demo illusion — 'It works' is the beginning of the sentence, not the end.

Receipts — The explanation is not the evidence.

Check one at random — Check one at random. Every time.

The fake test — A check that a fake could pass is checking nothing.

"It sounds right" — 'It sounds right' is the most expensive sentence in AI-assisted work.

04Guard

The blast radius — Radius, not language or framework, decides how much process you owe.

Managed vibe coding — Vibe coding is one gear in a car that also has brakes, mirrors, and a licence.

The five gates — You cannot read every line. You can refuse to ship without a gate.

Commission the gate — A gate nobody understands gets disabled.

Tune the budget, keep the gate — Widening the gate to pass a change is the exact move this deck exists to prevent.

The flight recorder — One wrong move ruins nothing, if everything is recorded.

The three secrets — An unused key is an unlocked door.

Slopsquatting — The agent's spelling is not evidence.

05Taste

The taste test — 'Best' is not objective. That is precisely why they need you.

CRITIQUE.md — Take responsibility for a position, in writing, with reasons that survive your absence.

The four standing questions — Delegate the menu. Keep the choosing.

The last 20% — The first 80% is a demo. The last 20% is the product.

06Run

Decomposition — Big things are not built in one go. They are decomposed, dispatched, and verified.

One idea per commit — Commit messages are the department's testimony.

The reviewer with fresh eyes — Fresh eyes are not a compliment; they are a control.

The staff turnover — The model is staff, and staff turn over.

Ship something true — You do not need permission to ship. You need evidence it is true.
